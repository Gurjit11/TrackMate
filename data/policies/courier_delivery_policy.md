# SwiftCourier Delivery Policy

## 1.1 Estimated delivery date
Every shipment shows an estimated delivery date. Delays are measured in whole days beyond that date.

## 1.2 Normal delay window
A shipment delayed by up to 3 days beyond its estimated delivery date is within the normal delay window. The courier gives status updates only and no claim can be raised.

## 1.3 Significant delay
A shipment delayed by more than 3 days beyond its estimated delivery date is significantly delayed. It may be escalated to the courier support desk.

## 2.1 Failed delivery attempt
If the recipient is unavailable, the courier makes up to 2 more delivery attempts. The recipient can reschedule by giving a preferred delivery date and a contact phone number.

## 2.2 Incomplete address
If the address is incomplete, the courier holds the shipment for up to 5 days. The recipient must provide the corrected full address, the pincode and a nearby landmark.

## 3.1 Customs hold within limit
International shipments may be held for customs clearance for up to 5 days. During this time only status updates are given.

## 3.2 Customs hold exceeded
A customs hold longer than 5 days must be escalated to the courier support desk.

## 3.3 Customs documents
If customs asks for documents, the recipient must upload a valid ID and the purchase invoice. The hold continues until the documents are provided.

## 4.1 Lost in transit
A shipment with no tracking scan for 7 or more consecutive days is treated as lost in transit and must be escalated.

## 4.2 Delivered but not received
If a shipment is marked delivered but the recipient has not received it, the recipient must wait 48 hours after the delivery scan and check with neighbours and building security. After 48 hours a not-received claim can be filed.

## 4.3 Damaged on delivery
Damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery with photos. Damaged deliveries are escalated to the courier support desk.

## 5.1 Label created
A shipment with status label created has not yet been picked up. Tracking scans begin after pickup, and no delay is counted until the estimated delivery date has passed.
