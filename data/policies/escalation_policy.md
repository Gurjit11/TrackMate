# TrackMate Escalation Policy

## 1.1 When to escalate
Escalate when a shipment is lost in transit, significantly delayed, damaged, delivered but not received after 48 hours, or on a customs hold longer than 5 days.

## 1.2 What an escalation must contain
An escalation message must include the tracking ID, the last known tracking event, a clear description of the problem, and the policy clause relied on.

## 2.1 When to ask for information
Ask the user for information when the tracking ID is missing or not found, the address is incomplete, the recipient was unavailable, or customs documents are required.

## 2.2 When to give an update
Give an update when the shipment is moving normally or is delayed within the normal delay window of 3 days.

## 3.1 Weather delays
Severe weather such as heavy rain or strong wind can explain a delay. It does not change the delay thresholds or the escalation rules.

## 4.1 Human approval required
No message is sent on the user's behalf without the user's explicit approval.

## 4.2 Do not invent facts
If tracking data or policy information is missing, say so and ask for it. Never guess dates, locations or policy rules.

## 4.3 Cite the policy
Every statement about a rule or deadline must cite the clause it comes from.
