# ShopSphere Refund Policy

## 1.1 Refund eligibility
An order is eligible for a refund if the shipment is lost in transit, or if it is more than 10 days past the original estimated delivery date.

## 1.2 Non-receipt claim first
For a refund because the item was not received, a not-received claim must first be filed with the courier.

## 1.3 Damaged items
For items that arrive damaged, the customer can choose a refund or a replacement if reported within 7 days of delivery with photos.

## 2.1 Refund processing time
Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.

## 2.2 Refund method
Refunds are paid to the original payment method.

## 3.1 Cancellation before shipping
An order can be cancelled for free before the shipment is picked up by the courier.

## 3.2 Cancellation after shipping
Once a shipment is in transit it cannot be cancelled. The customer can refuse delivery and the order is then returned.

## 4.1 Refund requests while delayed
A refund cannot be approved only because a shipment is late, unless it is more than 10 days past the original estimated delivery date.
