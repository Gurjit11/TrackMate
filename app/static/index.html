RAG

Why TrackMate Excels in Production

Engineered with safety, grounding, and human-in-the-loop control.

Deterministic Guardrails (G1–G5)

AI language models can hallucinate policies or propose rogue actions. TrackMate wraps the LLM with deterministic Python guardrails ensuring no citation is hallucinated and claims strictly follow operating clauses.

Live Open-Meteo Weather (M5)

Real-world weather delays require evidence. TrackMate queries live Open-Meteo historical APIs for destination cities to explain transit hiccups without altering contractual escalation boundaries.

Human-in-the-Loop Dispatch

When escalation is required, TrackMate drafts the exact courier support desk email or WhatsApp customer message. A human supervisor approves with one click before anything is written to the outbox.
